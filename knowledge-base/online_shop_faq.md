# Online Shop FAQ

> Demo FAQ for the learning repository. Replace these sample policies with your organization's real approved content before production use.

## Orders

### How can I track my order?
Once an order ships, the customer receives an email with a tracking link. Signed-in customers can also open **My Orders** to view available tracking information.

### Can I change an order after placing it?
Order changes are not guaranteed after submission. Contact human support as soon as possible if a shipping address or order detail needs to be corrected.

## Shipping and Delivery

### Where do you ship?
We ship to most countries and regions that are available during checkout. If an address or destination is not available in checkout, shipping is not currently offered to that location.

### How much does shipping cost?
Shipping cost is calculated at checkout based on destination and selected delivery speed. Eligible shipping promotions are applied automatically.

### How long does delivery take?
Estimated delivery times are shown during checkout and in the shipping confirmation email. Order processing typically takes 1–2 business days before dispatch.

### Do you offer drone delivery?
The FAQ does not list drone delivery as an available shipping method.

## Returns and Refunds

### What is the return policy?
For this demo project, eligible unused items may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items should be returned in their original condition. Replace this sample policy with the actual approved return policy before production use.

### When will I receive a refund?
After an approved return is received and processed, refunds are issued to the original payment method. Processing time may vary by payment provider.

## Payments

### What payment methods do you accept?
This demo FAQ supports major credit and debit cards and supported digital wallet methods shown at checkout. The available methods displayed during checkout are the authoritative list.

### Why was my payment declined?
A payment can be declined by the payment provider for several reasons. Confirm the billing information and contact the payment provider or human support if the issue continues.

## Human Support

If the FAQ does not contain enough information to answer a customer question, do not invent a policy. Direct the customer to the configured human-support channel.
